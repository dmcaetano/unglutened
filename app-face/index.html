Quick guide

The Bristol stool scale

It’s a simple clinical scale for describing stool consistency. Types 3 and 4 are generally considered the comfortable middle; one unusual day isn’t a diagnosis.

1–2 Firmer 3–4 Comfortable 5–7 Looser
